Hand-guide turns torque off so you can pose the arm by hand. Hold it first: it drops without torque. The stall check stops the arm when a joint stays well short of its goal without moving, which usually means something is in the way.

Max reach

The green slice on the floor. The tool tip (the target point: the flange centre, or the attachment's tip) must stay inside it; the rest of the arm may cross its edges. Poses that take the tip outside are refused like a collision, on this page and by the backend. Directions are seen from above: 0° is the front, where the flange points at the zero pose (away from the Pi's ports); −90° is the arm's right.

Calibration

In hand-guide mode, pose the arm like the sim's zero pose, then set the zero. Bend each joint by hand: if the green pose turns the opposite way, reverse that joint.

Collision

The attachment is checked as a cylinder of its size along the flange axis: against the table (only its tip may come close), the base and shoulder, and the upper arm and forearm, so the wrist can't fold it back into the arm. Solving, the Motion target and playback all use it, and so does the backend once connected. The target becomes the attachment's tip.

Playback speed1×

Playback moves to the start pose at Max speed from Motion, then follows the recording. With the timing kept, each joint gets the speed it needs (up to 150°/s); otherwise every joint uses Max speed and may lag. The whole path is collision-checked before it starts. When connected it runs on the arm itself, so it keeps going if you close this page. Stop or Esc ends it, and so does moving the target.
